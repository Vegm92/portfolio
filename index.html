    About me

    I build it, ship it, and run it.

    I'm Victor — a full stack developer building AI solutions in Barcelona, freelance since 2022. I spend my time in the gap between systems: REST APIs, webhooks, OAuth, job queues and AI services, wired into pipelines that keep running when nobody is watching them.

    Day to day that's Node.js and TypeScript on the backend, SvelteKit or React on the front, PostgreSQL and Drizzle underneath, and Gemini or the Claude SDK where a model genuinely beats a rule. Mise en Place is where all of it comes together — I own that one end to end, from the schema to the Stripe webhook to the on-call.

    Before and alongside the code I've been the client-facing side too: I delivered a sales enablement app for BYD's European training, and I lead the operations team at premium EV brand experience events — where I built the real-time fleet-tracking app my own crew used in the field across a 21-vehicle fleet. Trained at ISDI Coders in 2021. Spanish native, English professional.
